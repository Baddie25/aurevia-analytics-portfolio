# Project 2 — Market Entry Strategy
## Market Evidence Interpretation

### Purpose

This review combines the current macroeconomic comparison with the available broad-skincare market evidence. It supports market screening, not a final entry decision.

### Evidence coverage

- Candidate markets in the macroeconomic dataset: 8.
- Markets with skincare figures in the current comparison: 5.
- Markets without comparable skincare figures: 3.
- Macro indicators describe economic context; they do not measure Aurevia's addressable category demand.

### Time alignment and comparability

- Macroeconomic context uses 2025 observations. Skincare market values refer to 2024, while historical value CAGR covers 2019-2024. These indicators describe different periods and should not be interpreted as perfectly time-aligned.
- The five skincare estimates are based on publisher summaries. Full report methodologies have not been reviewed, so category definitions, retail-value basis, exchange-rate treatment and cross-country comparability still require verification.
- Market-value CAGR describes changes in reported market value, not necessarily changes in physical sales volume. The current dataset does not establish the contribution of price, volume, product mix, inflation or exchange rates.

### Cross-market conclusions

1. Economic scale and population provide context, but do not establish skincare demand or Aurevia's likely market share.
2. GDP per capita and PPP are broad affordability proxies; they do not measure the income or spending behaviour of Aurevia's target customers.
3. Market value and value CAGR should be assessed separately. A large market can have declining value, while a smaller market can be growing.
4. Value growth can reflect changes in volume, price, product mix, inflation and exchange rates. These are factors to investigate, not explanations established by the current data.
5. Poland, Saudi Arabia and Chile remain evidence gaps in the broad-skincare comparison. Missing public evidence is not evidence of weak market attractiveness.
6. The current publisher summaries have not been validated against the full report methodologies. Verify category definitions, retail-value basis, observation periods and currency treatment before relying on cross-country differences.

### Next research priorities

1. Resolve or explicitly retain the three broad-skincare evidence gaps.
2. Verify category definitions and source methodology for the five existing market estimates.
3. Investigate volume growth and price/mix effects, especially where reported value growth is negative.
4. Map the category evidence to Aurevia's actual product categories and target customer segments.
5. Research competitors, regulation, channels and local operating requirements before modelling entry economics.
6. Use validated market, cost, investment and risk evidence before developing an investment decision.
