
# Aurevia Group — Company Profile

## 1. Company Overview

Aurevia Group is a fictional global consumer and retail company headquartered in London, United Kingdom.

The company develops, distributes, and sells consumer products across multiple markets, channels, and customer segments.

## 6. Business Model

Aurevia's commercial model follows this chain:

Products → Customers → Channels → Markets → Revenue

The Project 1 performance model focuses on:

Gross Revenue → Discounts → Net Revenue → Product Cost → Gross Profit

Returns are modelled separately and are used to assess returned units, refund value, and estimated gross-profit impact.

The broader business model extends beyond Project 1 into:

Gross Profit → Operating Costs → Contribution → Cash Flow

These additional financial measures will be introduced in later portfolio projects as the finance data domain is developed.

## 7. Business Timeline

### 2022 — Foundation
Aurevia operates primarily in Europe with a smaller product portfolio and relatively stable margins.

### 2023 — Expansion
The company expands into North America and increases its e-commerce presence. New products and marketing initiatives are introduced.

### 2024 — Growth
Revenue increases significantly. The Wellness division grows, and North America becomes strategically important.

### 2025 — Growth With Pressure
Revenue continues to rise, but margins decline because of:

- Increased discounting
- Higher logistics costs
- Product-mix changes
- Customer acquisition costs
- Inventory pressure
- Customer concentration

### 2026 — Strategic Decision Point
Management must determine how to sustain profitable growth, allocate resources, manage risk, and prioritize future investments.

## 8. Project 1 Executive Assignment

Aurevia has grown rapidly. Management needs to understand where growth comes from, whether it is profitable, which customers and products create value, and where leadership should focus next.

## 9. Executive Questions

1. Is the company growing?
2. Is growth profitable?
3. Which regions, divisions, and channels drive growth?
4. Which customers and products generate profit?
5. Are discounts helping or hurting performance?
6. Where should management invest, improve, or reduce activity?

## 10. Analytical Scope

Project 1 focuses on enterprise performance using:

- Commercial and transactional sales data
- Product cost and gross-profit measures
- Discount and pricing-related transaction measures
- Returns and refund data
- Illustrative performance targets
- SQL analysis
- Python diagnostics and validation
- Power BI reporting
- Executive decision recommendations

The broader Finance and Pricing data domains will be developed progressively in later portfolio projects.

## 11. Key Assumptions

This is a fictional business environment designed for analytical and strategic learning.

Business patterns, financial figures, customer behavior, and operational outcomes are simulated and documented as the data universe is developed.

Project 1 uses a deliberately scoped analytical model. Not all business processes described in the broader company profile are represented in the initial database.
